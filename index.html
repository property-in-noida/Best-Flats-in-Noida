How is the AI Ecosystem Expanding in Noida?

Uttar Pradesh is encouraging investments in AI, data centres and IT infrastructure to strengthen its digital economy.

Some notable investment proposals include:

  • AM Green Group: Proposed USD 25 billion investment towards a 1 GW AI compute hub in Greater Noida.
  • Sify Technologies: Proposed ₹1,600 crore investment in an AI-ready data centre and AI City in Noida.
  • SA Technologies: Proposed investment of ₹100–200 crore for a Global Capability Centre.

These developments indicate growing interest in the region's technology infrastructure. However, proposed investments should not be confused with completed projects or confirmed employment numbers.

As this ecosystem develops, Noida could attract more technology companies and skilled professionals, potentially creating additional housing demand.
